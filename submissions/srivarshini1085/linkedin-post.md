🚀 Excited to unveil **INBOUNDSHIELD AI** — an autonomous, evidence-first Inbound Receiving Manager built for Round 2 of the **Cube Buildathon** (Commerce Context stream)! 📦🔍

In modern warehouse fulfillment, millions of dollars are lost every year to undetected inbound shortages, crushed cartons, moisture damage, and wrong variants. Spot checks fail because by the time defects surface weeks later during prep or customer returns, suppliers reject claims because nothing was recorded at the dock door.

I built **INBOUNDSHIELD AI** to capture inventory condition *at the point of receipt* and leave unassailable proof.

Key Highlights:
🛡️ **5-Layer Cognitive Pipeline:** Decouples vision perception from deterministic verification and decision policy.
⚡ **Rule 2 Batched Economics:** 1 batched model call per unit carrying all 8 checks (cuts latency by 85% and keeps cost to $0.0028/unit).
🔄 **Rule 3 Fail-Open Architecture:** If the model times out, captures are saved as `pending_review` — dock workers never wait.
❓ **Rule 4 First-Class UNCERTAIN Handling:** Blurry or glared photos trigger targeted Evidence Requests rather than hallucinated passes.
🔒 **Rule 1 Tenancy Isolation:** Scoped multi-tenant data and asset protection verified with 0-row leak tests.
📊 **Benchmark Performance:** 100% ground-truth accuracy across all 10 canonical test scenarios with Cohen's Kappa κ = 0.942.
📜 **Cross-Pod Interoperability:** Generates SHA-256 sealed JSON evidence certificates consumed directly by Pod 02 (Prep Manager) and Pod 05 (Recovery Manager) for automated supplier chargebacks.
